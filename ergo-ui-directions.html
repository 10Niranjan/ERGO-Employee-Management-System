Where I'd go

A recommendation, since you asked for options

System
01 Aurora Glass. It's the only direction that costs you almost nothing — your tokens are already a dark indigo palette on #0f1117. It's a glass layer, three blurred orbs and a stagger helper on top of what you have, not a rewrite.
Tables
Steal 02's table. Hairline rules, tabular figures, uppercase micro-labels and no row striping. Drop that treatment into Aurora's dark panels and Attendance / Salary History stop looking like spreadsheets in a dark theme.
One screen
03 Ops Console for a live attendance wallboard. If a screen ever goes on a wall in the office, this is what it should look like. It would be punishing across nine admin pages.
Skip unless
04 only if the employee-facing app wants a separate, friendlier identity from admin. 05 only for payroll documents and exports, where big serif figures actually earn their keep.

Every mockup here is CSS on the markup structure you already have — sidebar, top bar, hero, KPI grid, data panel. Nothing needs a UI kit or an animation library. Hover the swatches to read hex values, and use "Replay motion" to watch a direction's entrance sequence again.
